ℹ️ How can I help you?

I am the MSP Support Bot — your AI-powered IT helpdesk assistant. I search through real support ticket history to answer your questions, and fall back to OpenAI for anything not in the database.

🎯 Try asking me about...

🔑 Password reset 📶 WiFi issues 💻 Slow computer 🖨️ Printer problems 📧 Email issues 🔒 VPN issues ⚡ Power issues 💬 Teams setup ⌨️ Keyboard issues 🖧 Network issues

⚙️ How it works

  • 1You type your IT support question
  • 2I search 1,137+ real support tickets from Autotask
  • 3If a match is found, I give you the resolution from the ticket
  • 4If no match, I use OpenAI to give a general IT answer
  • 5A reference ticket is shown so you can dig deeper if needed

💡 Tips for best results

• Be specific — "laptop keyboard not working" works better than "laptop issues"
• Describe what happened — "WiFi was working but suddenly stopped"
• Mention the device or software — "Outlook not syncing on Windows 11"
